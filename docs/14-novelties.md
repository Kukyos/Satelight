# Novelties — what this does beyond the brief

The rule for this file: an idea earns a place only if the data already supports it, and
every number here comes from `13-eval-results.md` (generated by `python -m
satelight.evaluate`). Anything needing a source we do not have goes to `10-unsourced.md`.

N1–N3 were added to the harness on 2026-09-30; N4–N8 were built with the pipeline.

---

## N1 · Scored between a floor and a ceiling, as one number per depth

Hard rule 8 already puts every score beside climatology (the floor) and GLORYS (the
ceiling). The harness now also states where the model sits between them:

    gap closed = (RMSE climatology − RMSE model) / (RMSE climatology − RMSE GLORYS)

1 means as close to Argo as GLORYS, which assimilated those floats; 0 means no better than
climatology. It is computed per depth and per basin from the RMSE tables, never pooled.

What it shows (headline, `hybrid`): over the whole box it closes **0.75** of the gap at the
surface, about 0.6 through 20–75 m, and **0.13** at 300 m. In the Bay of Bengal the
thermocline is where it does best, **0.90** at 75 m. At 700 m and below the gap itself is a
few hundredths of a degree, so the ratio there is unstable and not quoted: read the RMSE.

## N2 · What a cyclone feeds on, scored against Argo

The theme is Disaster Management, and a cyclone forecaster reads heat, not degrees. From
each contender's 15 levels and from each Argo cast on the same 15 levels, the harness
computes:

- **D20** and **D26**, the depths of the 20 °C and 26 °C isotherms (linear between levels);
- **TCHP**, the tropical cyclone heat potential, the heat above 26 °C
  (Leipper and Volgenau 1972), in kJ/cm².

A cast whose profile never cools to the isotherm, or has a gap above it, has no value
(unknown, not zero, not a bound) and is left out for every contender alike.

TCHP RMSE against Argo, whole box: climatology **21.038**, Satelight **12.950**, GLORYS
**11.929** kJ/cm², with the model's bias **−0.573**. In the Bay of Bengal the model
(**13.695**) is level with GLORYS (**13.522**). D20 is harder: **20.419 m** against
climatology's 24.149 and GLORYS's 16.421, with a deep bias of about +10 m that fits the
thermocline warm bias in the temperature tables.

## N3 · A cyclone's cold wake, from satellites alone

Two cyclones fall inside the held-out test block: **Mocha** (Bay of Bengal, depression 9 May
2023, landfall 14 May) and **Biparjoy** (Arabian Sea, depression 6 June, landfall 15 June),
dates from IMD RSMC New Delhi. The harness compares the box-mean temperature 3 days before
the depression with 3 days after landfall, in the reconstruction, in GLORYS, and in
climatology (whose change is the season alone), and writes a map
(`data/figures/cyclone_*.png`).

- Mocha, surface: reconstruction **−0.70 °C**, GLORYS −1.39, climatology −0.00. Box-mean
  TCHP in the reconstruction **93.6 → 80.0** kJ/cm² (GLORYS 107.2 → 95.5; climatology
  82.9 → 87.6).
- Biparjoy, surface: reconstruction **−1.09 °C**, GLORYS −1.71, climatology −0.54. TCHP
  **100.3 → 82.6** (GLORYS 109.0 → 82.2).

The satellite-only model carries the wake in the right place and sign, at about half to
two thirds of GLORYS's amplitude: a smoother field, as expected from a model trained on
many days. It is a case study, not a validation; GLORYS is the training target.

## N4 · The reference is checked too

INCOIS gridded Argo is range-tested (Argo global range) before anything is scored against
it, and it is itself scored against the held-out Argo casts beside GLORYS at the same
1° / 10-day resolution. It agrees with Argo much less well than GLORYS does
(`03-limitations.md` L6), so the Argo casts carry the validation and INCOIS is reported
beside them with that caveat, not hidden.

## N5 · An embedding you can open, and a proof it can fail

The embedding is a saved daily file, not a hidden layer. It is judged by two tests fixed
before scoring, each against a control: seasonal clustering against the day of year alone,
and a linear probe for mixed-layer depth (never an input, never a target) against the same
probe on the raw inputs. The headline's embedding passes the first and fails the second;
`unet-abs`, chosen on the validation block to carry S4, passes the second (R² **0.351**
against **0.255**) and fails the first. The requirement stays open (`11-deferred.md` D-09),
and the deck says so.

## N6 · No leakage, by construction

Contiguous time blocks a month apart; the test block untouched until scoring; GLORYS never
an input; the headline named on the validation block before any test number exists. The
extended-window run and the no-currents-no-winds run are labelled as a comparison and an
ablation wherever they appear.

## N7 · Every observation with its paperwork

Every Argo level carries its QC flag, data mode and source file into the harness and the
viewer. **8,500** levels failing QC in the test block are counted, and casts with no
good level are counted as rejected, never dropped.

## N8 · Nothing invented below the sea floor, or between days

Cells shallower than a level are masked. A day with a missing input (CCMP has granules
with no wind) is logged in the manifest and announced in the viewer, never filled.
