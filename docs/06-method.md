# Method — what the system does, step by step, and why

No accuracy figures appear here; they are in `13-eval-results.md`, generated by the
harness. This document is the design, and each choice points to the limitation in
`03-limitations.md` that forced it.

## 1 · Ingest onto one grid (S1, S2, S3)

One `_fetch_*` path per source in `oceanembed/fetch.py`, writing
`data/cube/<source>/<year>.nc` on the common grid with a `provenance` attribute.

| Input | Product | To the 0.25° grid | To daily |
|---|---|---|---|
| SST | OSTIA reprocessed, 0.05° | exact 5 × 5 block mean | native |
| SSS | Multi-obs L4 (reprocessed), 0.125° | exact 2 × 2 block mean | native |
| SSH | DUACS C3S twosat `adt` and `sla`, 0.25° | pass-through | native |
| Currents | OSCAR final v2.0, 0.25° | half-cell shift (4-cell mean), labelled interpolation | native |
| Winds | CCMP v3.1, 0.25° 6-hourly | pass-through | mean of 00/06/12/18 UTC |
| Target | GLORYS12 `thetao`, 1/12°, 50 levels | exact conservative ½-1-1-½ stencil | native daily mean |
| Static | GLORYS `deptho` | same stencil | — |

How it is read:

- **Copernicus products** come from their ARCO time-series stores: one read per level per
  time chunk, packed integers decoded in float32.
- **PO.DAAC products** come as server-side OPeNDAP subsets of just the box.

Measured sizes and rates are in `05-data-sources.md`.

**Nothing is made finer than its source (L4).** Every cell is the area-weighted mean of
the native cells inside it, over the ones that hold water. OSCAR is the one exception:
it sits half a cell off the lattice, so it is shifted, and the provenance says so.

## 2 · The target at 15 depths (S6, L5)

For each of 0, 5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150, 200, 300, 500, 700 and 1000 m, the
target is built from GLORYS in three rules:

- **Most depths** are interpolated linearly in depth between the two GLORYS levels
  that bracket them. Of GLORYS's 50 levels, 27 are needed.
- **0 m** is GLORYS's top level (0.494 m). The SST input is never copied into the output.
- **Masking.** A cell is valid at a level when at least half its area is water at both
  bracketing native levels. Otherwise it is NaN, and so is everything below the sea floor.

The mask is static, so the model output carries the same mask and the loss ignores the
NaNs.

## 3 · The window and the splits (L2, L3)

The window runs 2010-02-04 → 2024-12-15:

- **Start:** the first day of a GLORYS time chunk, inside the SMOS era, so the salinity is
  satellite salinity.
- **End:** the last day of the reprocessed salinity, so no reprocessed-to-real-time join
  is needed.

The splits are three contiguous blocks, each a month or more from the next:

| Block | Dates |
|---|---|
| Train | 2010-02-04 → 2020-12-31 |
| Validation | 2021-02-01 → 2022-11-30 |
| Test | 2023-01-01 → 2024-12-15 |

The test block is loaded only by the harness.

## 4 · The embedding engine and decoder (S4, S5)

The input for one day is a 100 × 240 image with these channels:

- the eight satellite fields, z-scored with train-block statistics;
- latitude, longitude and log sea-floor depth;
- day of year, as sine and cosine;
- a sea mask.

With the `lags` option, each satellite field is also stacked as it was some days earlier.

The architecture:

- **Encoder.** Maps the whole basin to a **32-number embedding per cell**, plus a
  **64-number embedding per day** pooled from the deepest layer.
- **Decoder.** A per-cell MLP from the 32 numbers to the 15 depths. Everything spatial
  the model uses therefore has to pass through the embedding.

Two encoders are compared by the harness:

| Name | Architecture | Problem statement item |
|---|---|---|
| `unet` | Convolutional U-Net, three down/up stages, GroupNorm | 4a (CNN) |
| `hybrid` | Conv stem to ¼ resolution, a 6-layer transformer over all 1,560 patches of the basin, conv up-path with skips | 4e (attention-based hybrid) |

Training:

- **Loss:** masked mean squared error on per-level z-scored temperature.
- **Optimiser:** AdamW with a one-cycle learning rate.
- **Precision:** bf16 autocast.
- **Selection:** the checkpoint with the lowest validation loss.

Each run is fully described by `configs/<name>.toml` and its seed. The run directory
keeps the config, the normalisation statistics and the loss curves.

## 5 · Baselines

- **Climatology:** the day-of-year mean of the training block, smoothed over ±15 days.
  It is the floor.
- **Linear:** one ridge regression per cell, from that cell's eight channels and the day
  of year to its 15 depths. It knows today's surface but has no spatial context.

## 6 · Output (E4)

`python -m oceanembed.predict <run> <from> <to>` writes:

- **Temperature:** `data/output/daily/OceanEmbed_thetao_YYYYMMDD.nc`, holding
  `thetao(depth=15, lat=100, lon=240)` in °C (potential temperature). It carries CF
  attributes, the model config and the full provenance.
- **Embedding:** `data/output/embedding/<run>_<year>.nc`, holding the per-cell and
  per-day embeddings.

## 7 · Validation (S7, E5, L1, L6)

`python -m oceanembed.evaluate` scores every contender on the test block: climatology,
linear, each trained model and GLORYS.

**Argo casts.** The set is every core Argo temperature cast in the box.

1. Values are read adjusted first, and converted to potential temperature with TEOS-10.
2. Each cast is interpolated onto the 15 depths between QC-accepted levels only. It is
   never extrapolated, and never interpolated across a gap wider than max(25 m, 0.2 ×
   depth).
3. It is compared at the model cell and day that contain it.

Casts failing QC are counted, not dropped. A (cast, depth) point counts only where every
contender has a value, so all rows are scored on the same points.

**Metrics.** RMSE, bias, correlation, and anomaly correlation against climatology, at
each depth, for the whole box, the Bay of Bengal and the Arabian Sea.

**INCOIS gridded Argo** (ERDDAP `incois_argo_10d_VAM`, substituting for the unnamed LAS)
is compared at its own 1° / 10-day grid:

- our daily 0.25° output is averaged down to it;
- its in-situ temperature is converted to potential temperature with its own salinity.

**GLORYS is the ceiling, not a rival.** It assimilated these casts (L1).

## 8 · What the embedding holds (L10)

- **Seasons.** The per-day embeddings are clustered with k-means into four groups, and
  the groups are compared with the four monsoon seasons (NMI), beside a shuffled control.
  A second check asks whether each day's nearest neighbour in embedding space falls in
  the same season, beside chance.
- **Mixed-layer depth.** A linear probe maps the per-cell embedding to GLORYS
  mixed-layer depth, which is never an input and never a target. It is fitted on the
  validation block and scored on the test block, beside the same probe on the raw
  satellite channels.
- **Maps.** The first three principal components of the per-cell embedding are drawn as
  colour, one map per day.

## 9 · The proof of concept (E6)

`start.bat` / `start.sh` serves the viewer at `http://127.0.0.1:8026`. It shows:

- the eight satellite fields of the day;
- the embedding as colour;
- the reconstruction as a 3D cube over the Bay of Bengal, the Arabian Sea or the whole
  box, switchable to GLORYS, the error, or climatology;
- the held-out Argo floats of that week standing inside it, coloured by what they
  measured;
- a profile inspector for any float or any cell;
- RMSE by depth from the harness.
