## How it works

### 1:02 · `pipeline` · 26 s

*On screen: card: pipeline.*

> A convolutional encoder with a transformer reads each day's surface, and compresses every cell into 32 numbers: the embedding. A decoder turns those numbers into temperature at the 15 standard depths, from the surface to a thousand metres, and masks every cell shallower than a level. It trains on the GLORYS reanalysis, 2010 to 2020, and never sees GLORYS as an input.
